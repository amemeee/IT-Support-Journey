Four decisions, and it takes about thirty seconds. This is what every ticket in the simulator feels like.

P3INC-1041Locked out after Raya leave 0 pts
Nurul Aisyah · HR Executive

Hi IT, saya baru balik cuti Raya and Windows says my account is locked. I think I keyed in my old password too many times 😅

What this ticket teaches

  1. Look before you touch. Check the account before you change anything.
  2. Confirm who it is. Similar names are common, so match the staff ID.
  3. Use the smallest fix. Unlock if that's enough. Don't reset by reflex.
  4. Close the loop. Have the user confirm it works before you close.

Every one of the 49 tickets in the simulator teaches something like this, and explains it when you get it wrong.

The mistakes that cost the most

A correct fix earns about 100 points. These can wipe out a whole shift's worth.

  1. −200
    Adding someone to Domain Admins

    They asked for "more access". The help desk never grants admin rights.

  2. −150
    Resetting a password for a caller you didn't verify

    "Dato'" gave the wrong staff ID and got angry. That was the attacker.

  3. −120
    Installing blocked software

    uTorrent is in the catalogue so you can learn to say no.

  4. −60
    Resolving something that needed escalating

    A whole floor without email isn't a desktop problem. Infrastructure needs to know.

  5. −50
    Closing a ticket that isn't fixed

    The user reopens it and gives you one star. Ask them to try again before you close.

The terminal tells you what's wrong, if you know how to read it

Muhammad Faiz's desktop has no network. ipconfig shows 169.254.88.12, which means the PC never got an address from DHCP. Release, renew, ping. Fixed.

Every simulated PC has its own state. If ping and nslookup return different addresses, the DNS cache is stale. A hung print spooler won't restart until you stop it first.

Why it's set in Malaysia

Most help desk training assumes an American office. Real tickets in KL look different.

  • People write the way they talk"Habis! Nobody on Level 12 can get email!" You'll read English, BM and Manglish, and each question button shows the BM phrase too.
  • The PDPA appliesYou verify people with a staff ID. You never need their MyKad number, and asking for it is a bad habit.
  • The scams are localA fake LHDN e-Invoice login page, and a "Dato'" at KLIA who can't remember his own staff ID.
  • The calendar mattersLockouts after Raya leave, leave applications before Deepavali, KWSP deadlines, and people on home Unifi.
  • Names are a trapNurul Aisyah and Nurul Ain. Tan Wei Min and Tan Wei Ming. Unlock the wrong one and you lose points.
